## Coding Rules

- TypeScript only
- Use reusable components
- Do not duplicate components
- Keep business logic separate from UI
- Do not hard-code secrets
- Do not create unnecessary dependencies
- Do not change database schema without team approval
- Do not create new core entities without team approval

---

## AI Rules

AI must not:

- Claim certainty about a landslide
- Give emergency instructions beyond configured rules
- Invent sensor data
- Invent satellite observations
- Present synthetic demo data as real observations

AI output must include an explanation of contributing factors.

---

## Data Rules

During development:

Use clearly labelled mock/demo data.

Never present mock data as real-time government or satellite data.
